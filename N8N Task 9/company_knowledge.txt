# TechStore - Customer Support Knowledge Base

## 1. Company Information

Company Name: TechStore

Business: Online Electronics Store

Products: Smartphones, laptops, tablets, headphones, smartwatches, and accessories.

Customer Support Email: support@techstore.example

Support Hours: Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

## 2. Shipping and Delivery Policy

- Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days.
- Express delivery takes 1–2 business days.
- Delivery charges depend on the customer's location and selected shipping method.
- Customers receive a tracking number after their order has been dispatched.
- Customers can contact support if their order has not arrived within the expected delivery period.

## 3. Return and Refund Policy

- Customers can request a return within 7 days of receiving their order.
- Products must be unused and returned with their original packaging.
- Damaged or defective products are eligible for return inspection.
- Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days after the returned product is inspected and approved.
- Shipping charges are non-refundable unless the product was damaged or incorrectly delivered.

## 4. Order Cancellation Policy

- Customers can request cancellation before their order is dispatched.
- Orders that have already been dispatched cannot be cancelled through the normal cancellation process.
- Customers can contact support for assistance with dispatched orders.

## 5. Payment Methods

- Cash on Delivery (COD)
- Bank Transfer
- Debit and Credit Cards

Note: Available payment methods may depend on the customer's location.

## 6. Warranty Policy

- Products include a manufacturer warranty where applicable.
- Warranty duration depends on the product and manufacturer.
- Physical damage and unauthorized repairs are not covered under standard manufacturer warranties.
- Customers should provide their purchase receipt when requesting warranty assistance.

## 7. Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: How long does delivery take?

A: Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days, while express delivery takes 1–2 business days.

Q2: Can I return a product?

A: Yes. You can request a return within 7 days of receiving your order, provided the product meets the return conditions.

Q3: When will I receive my refund?

A: Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days after product inspection.

Q4: Can I cancel my order?

A: Yes, if your order has not been dispatched yet.

Q5: What payment methods are available?

A: TechStore supports Cash on Delivery, Bank Transfer, and Debit/Credit Cards, depending on location.

Q6: Does TechStore provide a warranty?

A: Products include a manufacturer warranty where applicable. The warranty duration depends on the product and manufacturer.

Q7: What should I do if I receive a damaged product?

A: Contact customer support and provide your order details and a description of the damage. The support team will guide you through the return inspection process.

Q8: How can I track my order?

A: You will receive a tracking number after your order has been dispatched.

## 8. Important Support Instructions

- Always respond politely and professionally.
- Answer customer questions using the information in this knowledge base.
- Never invent company policies, prices, delivery dates, or order information.
- If the requested information is unavailable, explain that the customer should contact the support team.
- Do not request passwords, card numbers, or other sensitive payment information.
- Do not promise refunds, cancellations, or replacements without confirmation from the support team.
